Classified Dossier

VALORANT

The lore behind the Protocol — a world fractured by radianite, supernatural powers, and an endless war for Earth's future.

01
The First Light

The Valorant universe was irrevocably changed by an event known as First Light — a global catastrophe of unknown origin that bathed the Earth in mysterious energy. This cataclysm unlocked latent abilities in a small fraction of humanity, transforming ordinary people into powerful beings called Radiants. Simultaneously, it scattered a rare mineral called radianite across the world — a substance of near-limitless energy potential that would become both humanity's salvation and its greatest source of conflict.

02
Radianite & Kingdom

In the aftermath of First Light, the mega-corporation Kingdom Corporation rose to dominance by monopolizing radianite extraction and distribution. While Kingdom powered cities and funded technological revolutions, its ruthless practices — environmental devastation, exploitation of Radiants, and concealment of dangerous research — bred deep resentment across the globe. Radianite became the lifeblood of civilization, but also a weapon: unstable, volatile, and coveted by every faction with ambitions of power.

03
The Protocol

To combat the growing threats posed by radianite misuse and rogue Radiant activity, a clandestine international organization known as VALORANT Protocol was founded. The Protocol recruits elite agents from every corner of the globe — both Radiant-gifted and technologically augmented humans — binding them under a single mission: protect the world from catastrophic radianite events. The agents, known by codenames, each carry their own backstories, loyalties, and personal stakes in a world that has never fully recovered from First Light.

04
Mirrored Earth

The deepest layer of Valorant's lore involves the discovery of a Mirror Earth — a parallel version of the world where First Light unfolded differently. The shadowy antagonist group Omega Earth seeks to drain the prime Earth of its radianite to save their own dying world. This interdimensional conflict places the Protocol at the center of an existential war — not just against human greed or rogue powers, but against an alternate civilization fighting desperately for survival at the expense of their own.
